BLACKMERQURY / LEGAL

PRIVACY NOTICE

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller is Mezei Szabolcs, sole proprietor, operating under the BLACKMERQURY LODGE business name and the BLACKMERQURY brand. Contact: hello@blackmerqury.art.

2. What information is collected

When you submit an enquiry, the form may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, preferred tattoo date, tattoo placement and approximate size, project description, and an optional reference link. The form also records your acknowledgement of this notice and the time the request was submitted.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Your information is used only to receive, assess and respond to your tattoo booking request, digital-art commission enquiry or motion-design project enquiry, and to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps).

4. Where information is stored

Enquiry records are stored in the BLACKMERQURY Supabase database hosted in the EU Central (Frankfurt) region. Access to enquiry records is restricted to an authorised BLACKMERQURY administrator. Email notifications are delivered through the existing Google Apps Script and the owner's Gmail account. These notification emails contain only the enquiry category and an internal request ID; the full enquiry details remain in Supabase. The website does not use enquiry details for advertising or mailing lists.

5. Retention

Enquiry information is retained only for as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up. If no contract or booking follows, the record is deleted when the enquiry is closed and there is no further legitimate need to retain it. If the enquiry results in a contract, relevant records may be retained for the periods required by applicable law.

6. Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or restriction of processing. You may also object where applicable and lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH). To exercise your rights, email hello@blackmerqury.art.

7. Voluntary information

Submitting an enquiry is voluntary. You should not include sensitive personal information in the project description or reference links. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking; an appointment is confirmed separately after direct agreement.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website or enquiry process changes. The current version will be available on this page.

Privacy enquiries: hello@blackmerqury.art
